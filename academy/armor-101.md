# 🛡️ Armor 101 — The Armor Production Landscape

*Armor is **72% of all recipes** (255 of 354). If you understand armor, you understand the guild economy.*

## The 6-slot system

Almost every armor family is a **6-piece set**: Helmet, Shoulder Pads, Arm Pads, Torso, Leg Pads, Gloves. A few families skip the gloves (5-piece). When you plan a set, you're planning 6 recipes that usually share the same material family — one mining run can feed a whole set.

## The Aramid ladder — the craftable spine

**Aramid** is the largest and most structured family (17 pieces). It's the tier ladder every member should know:

| Tier | Example | Needs (helmet) |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | Aramid Basic Helmet | titanium syntactic foam ×5 + textiles ×2 + rubber ×2 |
| **Modified** | Aramid Modified Helmet | carbon fiber + textiles + rubber |
| **Altered** | Aramid Altered Helmet | carbon fiber + textiles + rubber |
| **Tremor** | Aramid Altered Leg Pads | carbon fiber + textiles + cobalt |

- **Basic tier** runs on **titanium** (through titanium alloy → titanium syntactic foam) — the choke point from the [Mining guide](mining.md).
- **Modified/Altered** step up to **carbon fiber** (carbon ×2).
- **Tremor** adds **cobalt** — the top rung, only 2 mine sites (Kepler's Dome, DeMorgan's Castle).

## Dilatant — the dual-tier set

| Tier | Needs (helmet) |
|---|---|
| **Dilatant 46b** | carbon fiber + textiles + rubber |
| **Dilatant 50b** | titanium syntactic foam + textiles + glass + rubber |

50b is the premium line — it adds **glass** (silicon ×3 + chemicals + aluminum) to the titanium base. Same 6-slot structure, higher stats, longer chain.

## The 30+ named families

Beyond the craftable spine, the data lists dozens of full sets, most 6-piece:

- **Powered sets:** Delta Powered, Havoc Powered, Firstborn Powered, Legionnaire Powered
- **Manufacturer families:** Locans (Defense, Ethereal, Patrol, Pressured, Stabilized), Pythica (Special Operations, Mobile Infantry, S1, Sustained, Durable), NanoTech (Trauma, Scout, Voltaic, Cognizant, Vitality), PreMet (Buffer, Contact, Collision, Impact), Infensus (Minimalist, Shock, X1, Heavy)
- **Others:** MT-27 Ballistics, Tactical Systems, Advanced Civilian, XenoTech Expeditionary

Most of these share the standard inputs (carbon fiber / titanium syntactic foam + textiles + rubber) with per-family stat profiles. Check any item in the calculator for its exact recipe and stats — every piece carries armor/shielding/endurance/reflection/resistance numbers.

## Known gaps

- **XenoTech Expeditionary** (6 pieces) + **Portable Vortex Particle Emitter** need `ultra resilient mineral` — **no mine site yields it** (see [Mining](mining.md)). These plans currently dead-end at "no mine site." If you find where it drops, tell an officer — it unblocks 7 recipes.
- Families with **5 pieces** (no gloves): Tactical Systems, Advanced Civilian, MT-27 Ballistics, Locans Stabilized, Pythica Durable, NanoTech Vitality.

## Strategy notes

- **Set-craft, don't piece-craft.** Six pieces share materials; planning the set through the calculator's tray shares the stock ledger, so surplus from one piece feeds the next — the panel even warns you when per-piece planning would over-count batches.
- **The tiers are the roadmap:** Basic (titanium) → Modified/Altered (carbon fiber) → Tremor (+cobalt). New players can farm the Basic tier while stockpiling titanium; experienced players can push Tremor and Dilatant 50b.
- **Faction tags** (e.g. EC) mark recipe origins — some families may gate behind faction unlocks. Verify in-app.
