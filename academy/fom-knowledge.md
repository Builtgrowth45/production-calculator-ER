## 1. What the game is

**Face of Mankind (FoM)** — a first/third-person MMOARPG set in a dystopian 24th-century persistent universe. Developed by **Duplex Systems** (German studio, founded Jan 2001; story copyright 2001–02), designed by **Marko Dieckmann**, published by **Ojom**. Released **27 March 2006** (retail 31 Aug 2006). The tagline: *players have full control by creating their own rules, faction hierarchy and story.*

Core design pillars:
- **Fully player-driven** — faction politics, elected leaders, player-generated missions, a player-managed economy
- **Devoid of NPCs** — no quest-givers; objectives are auto-generated or faction-assigned
- **One seamless persistent world** — Earth cities + 13+ off-world colonies linked by the **Vortex-Net** (Vortex Gates)
- **Twitch shooter combat** + skill progression over time (no classes)
- **Permanent death** — run out of clones + Universal Credits and the character is gone forever
- **Closed economy** — all revenue came from player subscriptions; free accounts capped at rank 4

## 6. The wars (fom-lore war dossiers)

1. **The Marketing Wars (2307–2317)** — EuroCore + CMG vs American Enterprises vs Asian Coalition. Corporate militias: CMG's Colonial Security Force (tactician **Ian Trevise**); AE's **Enterprise Security Division** (ex-FDC + mercs). 14 Sep 2307: ESD bombs EuroCore's NYC HQ. 2308: martial law; DSS Yukon + DSS Hydra above Earth. 2310: AE President **Malcom J. Nelson assassinated** in Manhattan (Tar-5 Rifle, EuroCore tech). 2311: rise of **The Guardians** (red shirts); CMG joins EuroCore. 2312: **13 mafia families → Brotherhood of Shadows**. 2314: final battle — AE eradicated at its Chicago fortress (abandoned by Asian Coalition). 2317 aftermath: EC/CMG bribe their way clear; Dominion seizes AE production; Asian Coalition members flee to Vortex Inc. **The three clans (BoS, MotB, GoM) were born of this war.**
2. **Government Coup I / Dominion Civil War (2414)** — Nov 16: high-ranking officers hijack a Senate meeting, assassinate all Senators, promote the **Factional Assembly**, declare surviving Senators KOS. President **Kent Everest** (LED: James Fox, Jai Patel) vs FDC Generals **John Montgomery** + **Dietrich Schaefer**. Dec 10: Senator **John Malkovic (PSCW)** shoots General Schaefer with a smuggled **Zanathid pistol**: *"Traitors are sentenced to death"* — then: *"flamethrowers are much more difficult to conceal, therefore I could not burn the traitor and had to shoot him instead. Please understand my hands were tied."* GIS investigations → demotions, dishonorable discharges; pro-government victory.
3. **ECMG War / Second Dominion War (Jan–Feb 2415)** — EuroCore (President **Reefer**) + CMG (Directors **Max Max**, **James Di Griz**) demand then seize NYC + DeMorgan's Castle, Tokyo, Pax Prime, most colonies. Defenders (LED James Fox/Jai Patel, FDC Gen Chris Myo, VI CEOs Lionheart/Zophad, GoM Consul NullZero, BoS Harbingers Gary Storm/Baines) hold at **Aurelia** (Jan 3: all factions united; no clones left to produce) — narrow defender victory. VI retakes Necars Field; Dominion wins Kepler's Dome; EC takes Andromeda City, Necars Field, Titan Station in a day. Feb 17: Reefer declares victory; peace: EC regains the **Titanium Belt**, CMG annexes colonies.
4. **VIEC War / Third Dominion War (2416–2417)** — EC + VI (CEO **Leonidas**) form **VIEC** (infiltrated by terrorists "**Built**"). Seize CMG's Kepler's Dome + Ceres Delta; GoM grants CMG Pax Prime; then VIEC attacks the Dominion: Manhattan, DeMorgan's Castle, Tokyo (from BoS); takes GoM's neutral **Aurelia** (Free Trade Zone). At peak VIEC holds everything except Kepler's Dome + Pax Prime (MotB). Then **Reefer and Leonidas mysteriously disappear** (believed GIS) → VIEC collapses; GoM retakes Aurelia overnight; Manhattan liberated (battle at the **Sky Trade Center**); DeMorgan's Castle (renamed **EuroCastle**) falls. Surrender: EC + VI each keep two colonies. **Razzle Dazzle** → EC President; **Afrie Kasaki** → VI CEO. Reefer briefly returns, then vanishes; Leonidas never seen again.
5. **Coup II / Second Dominion Civil War (2424)** — General **Knives Aldren** (FDC) overthrows President **Liam Taylor**: orbital bombardments, release of most-wanted criminal **Cyrus McLane** from DeMorgan's Castle; EC sides with FDC, VI with LED. Taylor captured on Pax Prime (LED switches allegiance), jailed with GIS Director Terad Cowel. **Jason West** (GIS) appointed President. March: new **Dominion Codex** (consolidated Senate, stronger Security Council, reduced presidential powers, PM role created then abolished). May elections: CCP 32.8%, Order First 23.3%, PSCW 21.6%, Leftist Libertarian 14.4%, Elite Agencies 8%.
6. **Xenomorph War (Jul–Nov 2424)** — Xenomorphs from **Necars Field** (colonization provoked them) hit Titan Station; ally with human radicals the **Light Bringers** → Pax Prime bombed (Vortex Net severed). Pegasi 51 besieged by a Xenomorph mothership → Dominion plans to use **Ceres Delta's ION Cannon** → **Brotherhood seizes the cannon, fires on Paris; the cannon backfires and obliterates Ceres Delta**. Andromeda City, Kepler's Dome, Aurelia, Titan Station (2nd attempt), Earth itself fall or are attacked (Brooklyn withstood). **25 Jul 2424: FDC dissolved** — Gen Aldren resigns mid-defense, severs the DSS Yukon from the Vortex Net, flees with mercenaries after bombing **LED HQ on Ground Zero**. Survivors folded into a militarized LED. Vortex Inc reconnects colonies: **Kamitakada (Lower Tokyo)** after **11 years** offline (the **Net Virus**); Nov 2: Upper Tokyo (after **Darrian de Mistral** replaced **Phoenix Mask** as GoM Consul), Pegasi 51, Ceres Delta restored.
7. **Confederate Secession (Mar–Jul 2425)** — EC (President **Gianni Liebhart**) + BoS (Harbinger Terragon) declare the independent **Confederacy**. Seize the **GDS Yukon — renamed "The Arcturus"** — plus Ceres Delta, Pegasi 51 (Confederate capital), Necars Field; everything beyond the **Arcturus Divide** is Confederate. LED (Commissioners Sean McCarthy, Bad Munki) fights back, loses; The Arcturus changes hands weekly, sold to **MotB** (hostilities aboard cease). June: CMG withdraws; Bad Munki MIA; McCarthy + Senators captured at Necars Field. 8. **GSU Secession / War of 2426 (Jan–Mar 2426)** — Winter 2425: GoM (Consul **Xyber Whitechain**) campaigns against DeMorgan's Castle Warden **Monphat**'s atrocities (forced labor, torture, forced urine-drinking, shock therapy); LED spy ops on Aurelia → KOS vs GoM, Aurelia seized. ## 8. Game systems (classic mechanics)

- **Objectives**: auto-generated solo or group; reward XP + Faction Credits; kill X players / gather resources / patrol worlds / guard locations
- **Economy**: Faction Credits → UC at 4× daily payouts; **closed economy** — revenue came from player subscriptions
- **Ranks**: XP → ranks; promotions granted by higher-ranked players; Trainee → Rank 1 at 10k XP
- **World takeover**: hours of combat + hacking → ~1-hour final battle for the world, hundreds of players
- **Permanent death**: clones + UC to respawn; both exhausted = character death
- **Production**: EuroCore production discounts; raw materials → production elements → items (the calculator's whole domain)
- **Combat**: twitch shooter; damage types (ballistic, energy, aura, bio, xeno, destruction) — exactly what the calculator's weapon data models
- **Implants**: neural implants enhance cognition; **biocells** = energy
- **Cloning**: the neural core of cloning (and its destruction in the Great Collapse) is the in-universe engine of respawn/permadeath lore

## 13. Original background (2003–05) & official storyline

**The original (alien-centric) background** — the pre-2009 official story (fomportal.com archive):
> By the mid-24th century Earth's spreading population had become obvious, attracting the attention of a **very hostile and aggressive alien race**. Contact with the initial interstellar colonies was lost; special task forces were sent to investigate — and if necessary exterminate — the species presumed responsible. **Many of these soldiers were never heard from again** — the new enemy proved exceptionally resilient and much faster than mankind's forces. Meanwhile the clans form mercenary troops "in an effort to free the colonies from the alien occupation" and claim them under their own sovereign governance.

The later (post-2009) revision dropped the alien element from the background; the Xenomorphs return only as the 2424 invasion.

**2005 official storyline catalog** (16 GM-written articles, published Feb–Dec 2005): *Suffer the Little Children* (StarDawn), *The Lies We Believe* (timekeeper), *Traitors among us* (StarDawn), *Honor among Thieves* (Kalyirria Heliomn), *Spiderwebs.* (timekeeper), *Shock* (Kamikaze), *Shadow of heros* (Sharp), *A new Beginning* ([MorpheuS]), *The Trickster* (timekeeper), *Trapped.* (timekeeper), *STS Genesis Weapons upgrade* (Sharp), *When the darkness clears.* (timekeeper), *Spat back into society!* (Snowblind), *Moonbase Technical Issues* (Snowblind), *The Wanderer Returns* (Devereaux), *The State of the Dominion* (Devereaux).

## 14. Open questions & discrepancies

1. **2561 vs "123 years after the Fall":** ER marketing implies the Fall happened 2438; canon (Kvahs) says **2430**. Unresolved — flag, don't smooth over.
2. **2427–2430 gap:** what happened between the Book of Justitia and the Fall is undocumented in the fom-lore archive; the FotD-era GM-run story events were poorly archived. The Vault of Mankind is the intended home.
3. **FotD gameplay details** are thin ("a nearly entirely different game"); the ER team "honestly don't know much about Fall of the Dominion other than its the sequel" (Open-FoM's Bioxide has a FotD emulator).
4. **FDC founding year:** 2073 per faction canon (Codex era); war dossiers treat 2424 dissolution as its end.
5. **Solernus vs Selanius:** The Bard's claim (holds 2004 original docs) that the GoM founder's real name was Selanius vs official "Solernus." Community-disputed, unresolved.
