## Resource choke points

Materials with **fewest** mine sites are your bottlenecks — plan around them:

| Resource | Sites | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `mineral oil` | **1** (Necar's Field) | rarest mineable; only site in the game |
| `titanium` | 2 (Pax Prime, Pegasi 51) | base of titanium alloy → titanium syntactic foam → **Basic armor tier** |
| `diamonds` / `iridium` | 2 (Pax Prime, Pegasi 51) | high-value haul, shares the two richest sites |
| `platinum` | 2 (Pegasi 51, Necar's Field) | premium export ore |
| `gold` | 2 (Pax Prime, Necar's Field) | premium export ore |
| `coal` | 2 (Andromeda, DeMorgan's Castle) | fuel-ish bulk; only two dig sites |
| `iron` | 2 (Titan Station, Necar's Field) | structural bulk |
| `manganese` / `nickel` | 2 (Andromeda, Aurelia) | paired ores, only the CMG-adjacent pair has them |
| `chrome` | 2 (Ceres Delta, Kepler's Dome) | required for **titanium alloy** — an armor-chain gate |
| `cobalt` | 2 (Kepler's Dome, DeMorgan's Castle) | top-tier armor (Tremor) ingredient |
| `ultra resilient mineral` | **0** | used by 7 recipes (XenoTech Expeditionary set, Portable Vortex Particle Emitter) but **no site yields it yet** — those plans dead-end |

## Strategy notes

- **Titanium is the economy.** Two sites, and it feeds the entire Basic armor tier through titanium alloy → titanium syntactic foam. A guild that controls Pax Prime + Pegasi 51 effectively taxes everyone else's armor production.
- **The organic quartet** (Brooklyn / Manhattan / Paris / Tokyo) is the only source of organic material and most plasma. Paris is CMG-owned — mining there earns the faction cut.
- **Ceres Delta + Kepler's Dome** are the chemistry corner: chrome, chemicals, silicon, cobalt. Every refinement-heavy plan touches one of them.
- **DeMorgan's Castle** is the industrial workhorse: 7 yields including the only other coal + bauxite + caoutchouc cluster.
- Bulk ores (copper, silver, vanadium, water) are widely available — never plan around them, they're everywhere.
- In the calculator, every plan's **OBTAIN** section already lists which site to dig each material at, with per-unit UC costs. Use it; this cheat-sheet is for planning *before* you open the calculator.
